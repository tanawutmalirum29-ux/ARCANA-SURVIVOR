## Every independent chat / worker
1. Read governance on central `main`; read your exact `AP-ROLE` Issue (not another team's).
2. Verify role status is START_AUTHORIZED, inspect current project `main`, check role branch and owned paths. Never pretend another worker has read/accepted their Issue.
3. Implement only your owned files, add tests and a PR to main. Raise `API_REQUEST` in your Issue when you need main.js/storage.js/build/shared files or any other team's paths.
4. Report evidence, blockers and next steps inside the project Issue or PR; use `MN` for another real iteration of work, not an acknowledgement only.
5. **Do not merge to main. CEO is Integration Lead**, verifies CI and QA independently and merges in controlled order.
6. No real money, paid entitlements, online grant/server authority, leaderboard or public launch without their separate security/release gates.

If central RULES.md and this document conflict, central RULES.md takes precedence. `APPOINTED` and a branch existing are **not proof of an autonomous running agent**.
