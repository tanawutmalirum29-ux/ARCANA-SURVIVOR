# Cosmetic Preview Catalog — ARC-08 incremental scope

This phase expands the wardrobe to 8 **design previews**, not 8 purchasable assets.
- FREE, GOLD and ACHIEVEMENT are currently earnable offline demo entitlements: Apprentice, Ember, Moon.
- EVENT, SEASON FREE, CRAFT, PREMIUM PREVIEW and SEASON PREMIUM PREVIEW are **locked mock catalog entries** until secure eligibility and grant logic exists.
- No real-money checkout or premium granting, and local demo save sanitation actively excludes locked preview IDs.
- In-game player particles and friendly projectiles inherit visual cosmetic palette and rarity particle count, but no combat stat changes.
- All designs are simple procedural/placeholder effects. Full bespoke costume art, animations and ultimate VFX remain future ARC-07/ARC-08 deliverables.
- Low VFX reduces projectile glow and skips orbiting particle flourishes.
- Unit tests assert 8 unique cosmetic catalog IDs, power-free schema and restricted demo entitlements; browser smoke asserts 8 wardrobe items.
- Do not interpret appearance rarity as power. Production commerce remains blocked by ARC-12/ARC-13 and device QA.
